Walk into medical visits prepared.

LuscentVist is a simple, mobile-first organizer that helps patients, veterans, older adults, and caregivers keep appointment questions, medication lists, health notes, and vitals in one readable place.

Open the live app View demo assets See the code

Built around the appointment.

The app avoids diagnosis and clinical judgment. It focuses on the practical things people forget when care gets complicated.

📅

Appointment prep

Capture what to bring, symptoms to mention, questions to ask, appointment times, and optional local reminders. Entries can be edited or deleted with confirmation.

💊

Medication list

Keep names, strengths, doses, schedules, exact times, notes, and supported daily reminders organized in one readable list.

📝

Health notes

Record how things have been going over time and flag specific notes to raise at the next appointment.

📈

Vitals tracking

Store user-entered measurements with units and exact times. Blood pressure uses separate systolic and diastolic fields.

✨

Text helper prototype

The current build uses local, rule-based processing. Any future cloud AI would require explicit consent before text leaves the device.

🔐

Encrypted backup

Save a passphrase-protected backup of visits, medications, notes, and measurements, then preview and restore only missing records without overwriting current entries.

👁️

Readable by design

Large tap targets, high contrast, plain language, and a table-of-contents home screen make navigation less stressful.

Private by default.

LuscentVist is designed as a personal organizer, not a medical device. User records are stored locally on the phone. There is no account system and no cloud database for health information.

  • No account required
  • No cloud health-record database
  • No diagnosis or medication safety checking
  • Data stays on the user’s device
  • Optional backups are encrypted before the user saves them

The story

LuscentVist was inspired by time spent at the John Cochran VA Hospital, watching veterans navigate complex care systems and seeing how easy it is for important questions or details to get lost in the moment.

Congressional App Challenge submission focus

The project demonstrates mobile app development with Flutter and Dart, local encrypted storage, privacy-conscious architecture, accessibility-minded UI design, and practical problem solving for patients and caregivers.
